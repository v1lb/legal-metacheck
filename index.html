Frequently asked questions

Does MetaCheck detect AI-generated images from the image itself?

No. The current analysis looks for indicators in supported metadata. Metadata can be absent, edited or removed. Results are not proof of an image's origin or authenticity.

Does MetaCheck upload my photos?

The app processes the selected image on your device and does not upload the image itself. If you configure an API endpoint and explicitly send a JSON report, report data may be sent to that endpoint. Check the report and settings before sending.

Why does a cleaned image look different?

Some formats are re-encoded to remove metadata. This can change quality or format, and animation or special data may be lost. Keep an original backup.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the Pro or purchase screen in the app while signed in to the Google Play account used for the purchase. Make sure Google Play is available and connected. If the purchase is not restored, email support with the app version and a brief description. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I request a refund?

Refund requests for Google Play purchases are handled through Google Play under its current refund policies and applicable law. Use Google Play's purchase history or its official help flow.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with the steps to reproduce it, what you expected, what happened and any error message. Remove personal information from screenshots before attaching them.
