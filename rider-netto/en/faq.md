# Questions and answers

Short answers about how Rider Netto works.

## The basics

### What does the app do?
It keeps a record of your shifts and shows what you really earn: net per hour and per kilometre, after fuel, repairs, wear of your vehicle and unpaid waiting time.

### Is it connected to Glovo, Deliveroo, Just Eat or Uber Eats?
No. Rider Netto is an independent app. It does not log in to your courier account, does not read the courier app and cannot affect your account. You enter the numbers yourself or from a screenshot.

### Do I need an account or the internet?
No. There is no registration. The app works without the internet.

### Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and no paid features.

### Can I use it with several platforms at once?
Yes. In Settings, tick the platforms you work for and add your own. At the end of a shift you enter the amount for each one.

## Shifts

### How do I record a shift?
1. Tap **Start shift** when you go online.
2. Tap **Waiting at restaurant** each time you wait for an order, and tap again when you leave.
3. Tap **End shift** when you finish, then enter what each platform paid.

### I forgot to start the shift. What now?
Open **Shifts** and tap **Past shift**. Set the start and end time, the distance and the earnings.

### I forgot to end the shift.
After 12 hours the app asks whether you are still working. Choose **End and fix time** and set the real end time.

### Can I change or delete a shift?
Yes. Open **Shifts**, tap the shift, change it and save. The bin icon at the top deletes it.

### What is "waiting" for?
Time you wait at a restaurant is usually not paid. The app adds it up and shows how much of your shift it takes and roughly how much money it is worth. If you type the name of the place, Stats shows where you lose the most time.

## Money

### How is "net" calculated?
Net = everything the platforms paid (tips included) − your expenses − vehicle wear.

### What is "wear per km"?
The cost of tyres, chain, service and loss of value for each kilometre, without fuel. Set your own value in Settings. Fuel is entered separately in **Expenses**.

### Why can't I see hours per platform?
When you work with several apps at the same time, the hours cannot be split fairly. Compare platforms by earnings per order instead.

### Why are some hours empty in "Best times"?
An hour is shown only when there is at least 30 minutes of work in it. Otherwise the result would be random.

## Distance and GPS

### Why does the app ask for location?
To count kilometres during the shift. Location is used only while a shift is running. When there is no shift, the app does not use it.

### Does the app save my route?
No. It keeps only the number of kilometres. Coordinates are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

### The distance is zero or too small.
- Check that location (GPS) is on.
- Allow location for Rider Netto in the phone settings.
- Turn off battery saving for Rider Netto. On Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, Oppo and some other phones the system stops apps in the background.
- You can always correct the distance by hand at the end of the shift.

### Can I use the app without GPS?
Yes. Start the shift without location and type the kilometres at the end.

## Screenshots

### How do I fill in earnings from a screenshot?
1. In your courier app, open the screen with the earnings for the day and take a screenshot.
2. In Rider Netto, open the shift and tap **Fill from a screenshot**.
3. Choose the screenshot and check the numbers before saving.

### Is my screenshot sent anywhere?
No. The picture is read on your phone and is not saved by the app.

### The app found the wrong amount.
Tap the right amount in the row under the button, or type it. Recognition reads digits and Latin letters. Labels in Cyrillic, Arabic or other scripts are not read, so the app finds the total by the layout of the screen. Always check the numbers.

## Taxes

### What is the tax estimate?
It is for couriers in Italy with a partita IVA in the regime forfettario. Turn it on in Settings. The app shows roughly how much to set aside for the substitute tax and INPS contributions.

### Can I rely on it for my tax return?
No. It is an estimate. Real payments depend on advance payments and contributions you have already paid. Check the rates and the result with your accountant. You can change the rates in Settings.

## Your data

### Where is my data stored?
On your phone. If Google backup is on in your phone settings, a copy is kept in your own Google account.

### How do I move to a new phone?
Turn on Google backup on the old phone before you change it. On the new phone, sign in with the same Google account and restore the backup during setup.

### How do I send my records to an accountant?
Settings → **Export**. You get CSV files with shifts and expenses that open in Excel or Google Sheets.

### How do I delete everything?
Uninstall the app, or clear its data in the phone settings: Apps → Rider Netto → Storage → Clear data.
