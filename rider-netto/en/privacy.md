# Rider Netto privacy policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-13 · **Developer:** Puwer Studio · **Contact:** werk9886@gmail.com

## 1. In short

Rider Netto is a record of your work shifts as a delivery courier. Your records are stored on your phone. The developer has no servers and receives none of your data. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. It does not connect to delivery platforms.

## 2. What is stored on your phone

- **Shifts:** start and end time, distance in kilometres, vehicle type, your note.
- **Earnings:** amounts, tips and number of orders that you enter for each platform.
- **Waiting:** start and end time of each wait and the place name, if you type it.
- **Expenses:** date, category, amount and your note.
- **Settings:** vehicle, wear per km, platforms, appearance, language and tax estimate rates.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

## 3. Location

- The app asks for location to count kilometres during a shift.
- Location is used only while a shift is running. During that time a notification is shown. The app does not ask for background location access.
- Coordinates are used on the phone to add up the distance. They are not saved and are not sent anywhere. Only the number of kilometres is stored.
- You can refuse location and enter the distance by hand.

## 4. Screenshots

- You choose a screenshot in the system photo picker. The app has no access to the rest of your photos.
- The text is recognised on your phone with Google ML Kit. The picture is not sent anywhere and is not saved by the app.
- ML Kit sends Google technical data about its own work: device model and system version, app name and version, performance figures such as recognition time, image format and resolution, input and output size, and error codes. It does not include the picture or the recognised text. Google says it uses this data for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit and does not pass it to third parties. Details: [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

## 5. Notifications

The app shows a notification while a shift is running and a reminder if a shift lasts more than 12 hours. Notifications are created on your phone.

## 6. Backup

If backup is on in your phone settings, Android keeps a copy of the app's data in your own Google account, under Google's terms. The developer has no access to it. You can turn backup off in the phone settings.

## 7. What leaves your phone

- **To the developer:** nothing.
- **Export:** a CSV file is saved only where you choose.
- **ML Kit diagnostics:** as described in section 4.
- **Help pages:** links in the app open this website in your browser. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address, as any website does.

## 8. Permissions

- **Location (precise and approximate):** kilometres during a shift.
- **Notifications:** the running shift and the reminder.
- **Foreground service:** keeps counting kilometres while the screen is off during a shift.
- **Internet:** used by ML Kit for the diagnostics in section 4. The app itself sends nothing.

## 9. Deleting data and your rights

- You can delete any shift or expense in the app.
- To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in the phone settings.
- The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to provide, correct or delete. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR, write to the contact address.

## 10. Children

The app is made for working couriers and is not directed at children.

## 11. Delivery platforms

Rider Netto is an independent app. It is not connected to, or approved by, Glovo, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Uber Eats or any other platform. Their names are used only so that you can label your own earnings.

## 12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
