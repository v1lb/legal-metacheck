# Tips for everyday use

A few habits that make the numbers accurate and take a few taps per shift.

## During the shift

1. **Start the shift when you go online, end it when you go offline.** Time without orders is also work time. If you count only the deliveries, your hourly rate looks better than it is.
2. **Use the notification.** The **Waiting at restaurant** button is in the notification, so you can tap it from the lock screen without opening the app.
3. **Tap "Waiting" when you arrive, not later.** A few missed minutes on each order add up to an hour over a week.
4. **Name the place with one tap.** After the first time, the restaurant appears as a button under the field.
5. **Do not use the phone while riding.** Tap the buttons only when you have stopped.

## After the shift

1. **Take a screenshot of the earnings screen** in the courier app and use **Fill from a screenshot**. It is faster than typing and has fewer mistakes.
2. **Enter the number of orders.** Then the app shows earnings per order for each platform.
3. **Check the distance.** If GPS lost the signal, correct the kilometres by hand.
4. **Add fuel and other costs the same day.** Later it is easy to forget.

## Once a week

1. **Open Stats.** Look at net per hour, not at the total. It shows whether the week was better or worse than the last one.
2. **Look at "Best times".** Work more in your strong hours and think about skipping the weakest one.
3. **Look at "Waiting by place".** If one restaurant always costs you 15–20 minutes, it may be better to decline its orders.
4. **Compare platforms by earnings per order.**

## Set it up once

1. **Battery.** In the phone settings, turn off battery saving for Rider Netto. Otherwise some phones (Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, Oppo) stop counting kilometres when the screen is off.
2. **Wear per km.** Count what you spend in a year on tyres, chain, brakes, service and insurance, and divide by the kilometres for the year. Put the result in Settings.
3. **Platforms.** Leave only the ones you work for, so the shift screen is short.
4. **Google backup.** Turn it on in the phone settings so you do not lose your records with the phone.

## Money and taxes

1. **Set money aside every week.** If you turn on the tax estimate, the app shows how much. It is easier than finding the whole amount in June and November.
2. **Send CSV files to your accountant** once a month or a quarter: Settings → Export.
3. **Keep fuel and repair receipts.** The app keeps the amounts, not the documents.
